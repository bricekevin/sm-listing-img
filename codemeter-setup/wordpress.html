Set it up

Steps 1 to 3 are the same for both services.

Showing steps for:
1

Plug in and join its Wi-Fi

Power codeMeter with a USB-C cable. After a moment it shows STEP 1 of 2 with a QR code.

Scan that QR with your phone's camera to join the device's Wi-Fi, or join the network codeMeter-XXXXXX by hand in your phone's Wi-Fi settings (the exact name is on the screen).

2

Open the setup page

Once your phone joins, the screen turns green (STEP 2 of 2) and a setup page usually opens by itself. If it doesn't, scan the new QR or open http://192.168.4.1 in your phone's browser.

Tap Setup Device.

No page? Some phones hide it while private DNS or a VPN is on. Type http://192.168.4.1 into the browser yourself.
3

Pick your service and your home Wi-Fi

  1. Choose your home Wi-Fi from the list and enter its password.
  2. Under "Which do you use?" tap OpenAI Codex or Claude Code.

Codex The token box disappears. Tap Save. That's all for the setup page.

Claude Leave the page open. You'll paste a token into the Claude token box next (step 4).

4
Codex

Wait for the blue SIGN IN screen

After you tap Save, codeMeter restarts, joins your Wi-Fi, and shows a blue SIGN IN screen with a QR code and a short code like:

KQX4-7HRTM

The code is good for 15 minutes. A countdown at the bottom shows the time left.

Your phone drops off the codeMeter Wi-Fi when the device restarts. Make sure it's back on your normal Wi-Fi or mobile data before step 5.

5
Codex

Sign in to ChatGPT on your phone

  1. Scan the QR on the screen, or open auth.openai.com/codex/device.
  2. Sign in to the ChatGPT account that has Codex.
  3. Type the code exactly as shown on codeMeter, then approve.
You sign in on OpenAI's own page. codeMeter never sees your ChatGPT password.
6
Codex

"You're all set"

Within a few seconds the screen says You're all set and your 5h limit and Weekly limit appear. They refresh every 60 seconds.

If the code runs out first, the screen says Sign-in code expired. Press B for a new code.

4
Claude

Get your Claude token (on a computer)

On a computer with the Claude CLI installed, open a terminal (Terminal on Mac, Command Prompt or PowerShell on Windows) and run:

claude setup-token

It opens a browser to log in to Claude, then prints a long token starting with sk-ant-oat01-. Copy the whole thing. You only do this once.

5
Claude

Get the token onto the device, then Save

Pick whichever is easier:

  1. Paste it. Get the token to your phone (for example, email or message it to yourself), paste it into the Claude token box on the setup page, and tap Save.
  2. Scan it. Use the token QR helper below on your computer. It turns the token into a QR code that your phone sends straight to codeMeter.

codeMeter restarts and shows your Session (5h) and Weekly (7d) usage within about a minute. It refreshes every 60 seconds.

You're done. After setup, codeMeter runs on its own. No phone or computer needs to stay on.

Reading the screen

Two cards: your 5-hour window on top and your weekly window below. Each shows the percentage used, a bar, and when that window resets.

Pace: am I using it too fast?

The bar itself never changes color. Pace shows in the percentage number, the card outline and corner dot, and the thin time line under the bar (how much of the window has passed).

  • Normal colors: on pace.
  • Amber: you're using this limit faster than the clock.
  • Red: clearly overspending, likely to hit the limit before it resets.

Status dot (top right)

  • Green: connected, numbers are current.
  • Amber: still connecting, or briefly rate-limited. It sorts itself out.
  • Red: an error. See troubleshooting below.

Codex screens are blue with a >_ codex wordmark. Claude screens are orange. Hold A to see the Status screen, which names the service (for example "Codex (plus)").

Troubleshooting

Tap a question to open it.

It won't join my Wi-Fi ("WiFi connect failed")

codeMeter needs 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi with a WPA2 password. It can't join 5 GHz-only or WPA3-only networks. Most routers also broadcast a 2.4 GHz network, sometimes under a separate name.

Check the password, then hold C about 7 seconds to start setup over.

The setup page didn't open on my phone

Stay joined to codeMeter-XXXXXX and open http://192.168.4.1 in your browser. If your phone warns that the network has no internet, choose to stay connected. Turning off a VPN or private DNS for a minute can help.

Codex "Sign-in code expired"

Codes last 15 minutes. Press B for a new code, then repeat step 5.

B only gets a new code from this screen or the "Can't reach OpenAI" screen. While a code is still on screen, just use it.

Codex "Can't reach OpenAI"

The device is on your Wi-Fi but can't reach OpenAI's sign-in servers. It retries on its own; press B to retry now.

If it keeps happening, an ad blocker or filtering DNS (Pi-hole, AdGuard, NextDNS, a parental-control router) is likely blocking it. Allow auth.openai.com and chatgpt.com.

"Codex login expired" or "Claude login expired"

The saved login stopped working, so usage updates have paused. Hold B about 7 seconds (keep holding past RE-ONBOARD). Your Wi-Fi is kept.

  • Codex: the device goes straight to a new SIGN IN screen. Repeat steps 4 to 6. It normally renews its own login, so this should be rare.
  • Claude: the setup Wi-Fi comes back. Rejoin codeMeter-XXXXXX, run claude setup-token again, and paste or scan the new token.
The clock shows the wrong time

codeMeter sets its time zone automatically from your internet connection using ip-api.com. If you use an ad blocker or filtering DNS, allow ip-api.com, then unplug codeMeter and plug it back in.

The bars are blank or the status dot stays red
  • Hold A and check the Status screen: is Wi-Fi connected, and is the right service listed?
  • Confirm your plan is supported (see What you need). Codex on a Business or Enterprise workspace is not verified yet and may leave the bars blank.
  • Filtering DNS or ad blockers can block the service. Codex needs chatgpt.com; Claude needs api.anthropic.com.
How do I switch from Codex to Claude, or back?

Hold C about 7 seconds (keep holding past RESET). codeMeter wipes its settings and starts setup again. Follow steps 1 to 3 and pick the other service.

Does it update itself? Something went badly wrong

Yes. It checks for firmware updates every 6 hours and installs them over Wi-Fi. If the device won't start at all, a USB recovery flasher is at bricekevin.github.io/fw (use Chrome or Edge on a computer). Or email us and we'll help.

I lost the device or I'm giving it away

Hold C about 7 seconds to wipe it. If it's out of your hands, cut off its access from your account:

  • Codex: in your ChatGPT account's security settings (for example, log out of all devices).
  • Claude: in your Claude account settings, revoke the token.

Privacy

Your login stays on the device

Your ChatGPT sign-in or Claude token is stored only on codeMeter. There's no Encinitas 3D server and no account with us. The token helper on this page runs entirely in your browser.

What it connects to

OpenAI (Codex) or Anthropic (Claude) for your usage, GitHub for firmware updates, internet time servers, and ip-api.com for your time zone. Nothing else.
