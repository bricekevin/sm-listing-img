You'll need: a ChatGPT plan that includes Codex, your phone, and 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.

Verified on ChatGPT Plus. Business and Enterprise workspaces are not verified yet.

  1. Plug in and join its Wi-Fi

    Power codeMeter with a USB-C cable. When it shows STEP 1, scan the QR code with your phone's camera, or join the Wi-Fi network codeMeter-XXXXXX shown on the screen.

  2. Choose OpenAI Codex and your Wi-Fi

    A setup page opens on your phone. If it doesn't, go to http://192.168.4.1.

    1. Tap Setup Device.
    2. Under "Which do you use?" tap OpenAI Codex.
    3. Choose your home Wi-Fi, enter its password, and tap Save.

  3. Sign in with the code on the screen

    codeMeter restarts and shows a blue SIGN IN screen with a code like KQX4-7HRTM.

    1. On your phone (back on your normal Wi-Fi), open auth.openai.com/codex/device or scan the QR on the screen.
    2. Sign in to ChatGPT.
    3. Type the code and approve.

    The code lasts 15 minutes. If it expires, press B for a new one.

  4. You're all set

    Your 5h limit and Weekly limit appear within seconds and refresh every minute. codeMeter now runs on its own; your phone isn't needed.

Reading the screen

The top card is your 5-hour window, the bottom card your weekly window: percentage used, a bar, and when it resets.

The bar never changes color. Pace shows in the percentage, the card outline and corner dot, and the thin time line under the bar:

  • Normal colors: on pace.
  • Amber: using it faster than the clock.
  • Red: clearly overspending; likely to run out before it resets.

The small dot at the top right is the connection: green OK, amber connecting or rate-limited, red error.

Troubleshooting

It won't join my Wi-Fi

codeMeter needs 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi with a WPA2 password (not 5 GHz-only or WPA3-only). Check the password, then hold C about 7 s to start setup over.

The setup page didn't open

Stay on the codeMeter-XXXXXX Wi-Fi and go to http://192.168.4.1. If your phone says the network has no internet, stay connected anyway.

"Sign-in code expired"

Press B for a new code. B only gets a new code on this screen and on "Can't reach OpenAI".

"Can't reach OpenAI"

It retries on its own, or press B. If it keeps happening, an ad blocker or filtering DNS is in the way: allow auth.openai.com and chatgpt.com.

"Codex login expired"

Hold B about 7 s. codeMeter shows a new SIGN IN screen; repeat step 3. Your Wi-Fi is kept.

"Claude login expired"

Hold B about 7 s. The codeMeter-XXXXXX Wi-Fi comes back: run claude setup-token again and paste the new token. Your Wi-Fi is kept.

Bars stay blank

Check that you have a Pro or Max plan, and that an ad blocker isn't blocking api.anthropic.com.

The clock shows the wrong time

The time zone comes from ip-api.com. Allow it in your ad blocker, then unplug and replug codeMeter.

Switch to the other service

Hold C about 7 s to reset, then set up again and pick the other service.

Won't start at all

Updates install themselves. For a USB recovery, use bricekevin.github.io/fw in Chrome or Edge on a computer, or email us.
